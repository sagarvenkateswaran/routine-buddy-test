Privacy Policy

DigiMaa (“the App”) is published by Net Zero Strategic, LLC (“we”, “us”).

What we collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect names, voice recordings, or routines on our servers. Routines, the optional child’s name, language and settings are stored only on your device.

Microphone and speech

Voice mode is optional. When enabled, the device’s built-in speech recognition (Apple or Google) converts speech to text to detect phrases like “I am done”. Audio may be processed by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. We do not store or receive audio or transcripts. Touch mode never uses the microphone.

Location

The optional weather reminder asks for approximate location and sends rounded coordinates to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get the current temperature, and to BigDataCloud (bigdatacloud.com) to find the country so temperatures are spoken in °C or °F. Only the country code is kept on the device. We do not store your location. You can turn this off in the App.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check subscription status; it receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, not payment card details.

Children

The App is intended to be set up by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, show ads, or use third‑party analytics or tracking.
